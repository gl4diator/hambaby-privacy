HamBaby: My Hamster Pet – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

HamBaby: My Hamster Pet (“HamBaby”, “the game”) is a game for children, made by HamBaby Games (“we”, “us”). We care about children's privacy. This policy explains what information is used when the game is played and how.

In short: we do not ask for or collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no chat and no way for children to share anything with other people. Game progress stays on the device.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for, collect or store names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, photos, voice recordings or location. Children cannot create an account, talk to other players or post anything publicly.

2. Information stored on the device

The game saves its progress (for example coins, hamsters, unlocked rooms and settings such as music on/off) only on the device, in the game's own storage. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If a drawing from the drawing game is saved to the photo gallery, this happens only when the save button is pressed, the picture stays on the device, and the phone may ask for permission first. We never receive these pictures.

3. Advertising

The free version of the game shows ads through Google AdMob. Because the game is made for children, every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to ads suitable for all ages (rating “G”). This means no personalised (interest-based) ads and no remarketing.

To show ads, Google may process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address, the type of device and operating system, and the advertising identifier. This is used only for showing contextual ads, counting how often an ad is shown, measuring ads and preventing fraud, as allowed by children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and the GDPR). You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google treats child-directed requests.

Some ads are “rewarded”: they are watched only if a player chooses to, in exchange for a small in-game reward. Buying “No Ads” removes the other ads.

4. In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases. Before any purchase, a grown-up check (a simple maths question) is shown so that children cannot buy things by accident. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store any payment information. Google Play may tell us that a purchase was made so that the game can give the item.

5. Links outside the game

Links that leave the game (such as this privacy policy or the contact e-mail) are only in the “Parents” section, which is protected by the same grown-up check.

6. Service providers

  • Google AdMob – ads (see section 3).
  • Google Play – downloads, purchases and app updates.
  • Unity – the game engine and its in-app purchase system, which may process basic technical information (such as device type and app version) needed for the game and for purchases to work.

We do not sell any information and do not share information with anyone else.

7. Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child's personal information has reached us in any way, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Parents and guardians can ask us at any time what information we have about their child (normally none) and ask for it to be deleted. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union), you may also have the right to access, correct or delete data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
